# Reply template (adapt; keep 180-320 words)

Dear <first name>,

Thank you for your email and for reviewing the feedback so carefully. I have
looked at your <report/submission> again against the rubric.

<Paragraph 1 - the main issue that sets the band, tied to the brief or
rubric wording. E.g. a required component that is missing, and which
criteria it affects.>

<Paragraph 2 - the second reason, preferably built from what the student
already acknowledged: "the errors you acknowledge are in the core
calculations rather than minor details: ..." Name what is already in a
higher band if relevant: "Your Ethics mark is already in the Distinction
band.">

<Paragraph 3 (optional) - a brief, direct answer to any technical question
they asked, with one practical tip.>

Having reviewed each criterion, the marks remain as issued. If you would
like to discuss the feedback, I am happy to meet briefly; if you wish to
take it further, you may request a formal review through
[formal review process].

Kind regards,
<Lecturer name>

---
Variants:
- Clerical error found: "Having checked the record, your mark for <criterion>
  was entered incorrectly as X; it has been corrected to Y, and your total is
  now Z. The other marks remain as issued."
- Mark raised by the lecturer: state the new mark and the specific reason in
  one sentence; do not imply other criteria are under review.
- Non-appeal emails (extension, feedback question): same tone and length;
  give the answer or the next step in the first paragraph.
