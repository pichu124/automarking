# Checks before deciding

- Scale: convert issued marks to the scale the student quotes (e.g. app /100
  -> LMS points; a 90-mark group part is x0.9; per-criterion maxima from the
  rubric). A mismatch is often the student copying the wrong line.
- Band thresholds: quote the rubric's own descriptor words for the band
  the work sits in and the one requested.
- Claims: for each claim, accurate / partly accurate / inaccurate. Check
  the claim is in the SUBMISSION, not only in the appeal.
- Required components from the brief (e.g. "initial AND additional
  portfolio with balances", required screenshots, required Excel file,
  dates). A missing component usually blocks "includes all required
  components" (Presentation) and limits the analysis criteria.
- Original feedback: withdraw anything unfair (e.g. "too short" for a
  section inside the word limit) and restate the real basis.
- Images in reports are invisible when text is extracted - never tell a
  student a figure or screenshot is missing unless the lecturer confirms it.
